Resolved events

Markets are tracked after they leave the open listing and are re-checked with GET /markets/{ticker}. A binary result is shown only after the API reports status=finalized with result = yes or no.

The settlement time is the API's settlement_ts; if it is missing, this table leaves it blank and separately shows when the collector first observed the finalized result. See Kalshi's Market Lifecycle ↗ and Get Market ↗.
