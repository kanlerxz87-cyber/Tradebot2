Resolved events

Markets are tracked after they leave the open listing and are re-checked with GET /markets/{ticker} until the API reports result = yes or no. That result, and the settlement value when present, are shown here.

Resolution comes from the market object's result field (official docs ↗). Where the API returns an empty result, the market stays open on this list and no outcome is inferred.
