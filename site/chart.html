Chart & liquidity

YES contract price over time with seconds, minutes or hours on the X axis, plus the resting order book: how many contracts are available at each price.

Axes and units

X axis is time (labels in UTC; the data file timestamps are UTC). Y axis is the YES contract price in dollars, on a $0.00–$1.00 scale where $1.00 is a certain YES. Seconds mode plots individual prints from GET /markets/trades; minutes mode plots 1-minute candles and hours mode 1-hour candles from GET /series/{series_ticker}/markets/{ticker}/candlesticks. Dashed red lines mark recorded ≥ $0.10 alerts. Grey bars show contracts traded per period.

Candles and the trade tape are captured for the most active markets only (to stay inside public rate limits). Other markets fall back to collection-run snapshots, labelled “snapshot mode”.
