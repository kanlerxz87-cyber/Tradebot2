Portfolio & strategy lab

Automated paper trades opened from high-ROI alerts, marked against live YES bids, settled at resolution ($1.00 for YES, $0.00 for NO). Performance is broken out by strategy, topic and alert direction so trade types can be compared.

Strategy definitions

StrategyRuleRisk controlSource
follow_riseBuy YES at the ask after an alert with Δ ≥ +$0.10 that passes the high-ROI screen. 10 contracts; +$0.05 target or −$0.05 stop on the YES bid; 48h time stop. collector
fade_dropBuy YES at the ask after an alert with Δ ≤ −$0.10 that passes the high-ROI screen. Same bracket and time stop. collector
Resolution holdAny open paper position that reaches settlement is closed at the API result value. No leverage; 1 contract pays $1.00 or $0.00. GET /markets/{ticker}
