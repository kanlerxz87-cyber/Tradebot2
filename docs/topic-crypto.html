Two KXBTCD strikes quote 0.0000 on every field. That is not a zero probability — it is a market that had not traded when the snapshot was taken (volume 0.00, open interest 0.00, both sizes 0.00). The dashboard renders such markets with an em-dash rather than 0c, because a 0-cent price and "no price yet" are different facts.

Most crypto markets here settle from CF Benchmarks, not an exchange price. The rules text states it explicitly: the outcome uses the average of sixty BRTI prints in the final minute. A Coinbase or Google price can differ from that average and does not determine the contract.
