Unverified by construction

  • Any production price. The task targets the demo exchange; production is out of scope.
  • A real demo-account balance. No key existed in the build environment, so /portfolio/balance was never called and no balance is shown anywhere.
  • Liquidity of gas, election and culture markets at scale. At capture time their books held tens of contracts, so depth numbers describe the demo, not a tradeable market.
  • Whether Kalshi serves CORS headers for this Pages origin. The dashboard asks your browser directly and labels a snapshot when it fails; see the flag list for the exact test.
