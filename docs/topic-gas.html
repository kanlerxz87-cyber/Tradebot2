Why gas rows are thin — and one data trap. The demo exchange lists the gas ladder but barely trades it: three strikes of KXAAAGASD-28SEP16 were open with bids of 0.35, 0.41 and 0.00, total volume of 8.16, 12.98 and 3.17 contracts, and every one quoted a YES ask of 1.0000 because the NO bid side was empty. Do not read the derived ask (1 − no_bid = 1.00) as a real offer — it means "nobody is bidding NO", not "YES costs a dollar". One strike is worse: no bid, no offer, but a last trade at 0.90, so its last price is stale and unquotable. That is why the dashboard shows depth next to every price.
