The screen behind Chat 2 explicit, testable, not a validated model

For each recorded move the site computes, in cents per contract:

  • reversion target = 0.5 × previous price + 0.5 × new price (a 50% retrace of the move).
  • gross edge = |target − new price|.
  • cost = half the quoted spread + the official taker fee per contract at that price (ceil(0.07 × C × P × (1−P)), from the Kalshi fee schedule).
  • score = gross edge − cost. A move enters Chat 2 only when score ≥ 2¢ and price is between 5¢ and 95¢, spread ≤ 5¢, top-of-book size ≥ 20 contracts, 24h volume ≥ 20, and open interest ≥ 1.

Honesty note. This is a hypothesis stated up front so it can be tested, not a proven edge. Every paper trade opened from Chat 2 is recorded on the portfolio page, where its realised result is what actually judges the idea.
