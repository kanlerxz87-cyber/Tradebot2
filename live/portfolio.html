How to read this

  • These are not predictions of live performance. The demo exchange is a test environment; fills are simulated at the quoted price with no slippage.
  • Fee-aware. Taker fee per fill = ceil(0.07 × C × P × (1−P)) from the official schedule. Maker fees are not modelled because a simulation cannot know queue position.
  • Resolution carry buys contracts already trading ≥85¢. It wins often and small; a single loss can outweigh many wins.
  • Cheap tail buys ≤15¢ movers. It loses often and small and relies on rare large wins.
  • Before drawing conclusions, let auto-trade run across several days and read the per-strategy table — not the headline P&L.
