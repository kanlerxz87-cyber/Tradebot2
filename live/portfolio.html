How to read this

  • These are not predictions of live performance. The demo exchange is a test environment; entries are capped by visible side-ask size and exits use the side bid; no queue, slippage or market-impact model. At most 10 open positions / $100 entry cost per fill. Closed markets cannot simulate market exits; settlement requires finalized status. Browser-local storage is not an always-on broker.
  • Fee-aware. General-fee scenario: M=1, 0.07 × C × P × (1−P), rounding fee + position cost up to a centicent ($0.0001). Series/event overrides are not verified. No settlement fee. Maker fees are not modelled because a simulation cannot know queue position.
  • Resolution carry buys contracts already trading ≥85¢. It wins often and small; a single loss can outweigh many wins.
  • Cheap tail buys ≤15¢ movers. It loses often and small and relies on rare large wins.
  • Before drawing conclusions, let auto-trade run across several days and read the per-strategy table — not the headline P&L.
