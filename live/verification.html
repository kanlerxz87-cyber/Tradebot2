Capture and release evidence

  • Small dated browser capture: per-record official URL, original observation time, input SHA-256, selection time, and Actions re-verification URL when available. It is not live data.
  • Full upstream capture and CORS / unsigned-WebSocket probe. Inspect their timestamps and errors before use.
  • Release verification record and GitHub Actions runs. Unit/DOM tests use labeled fixtures; only runner/API evidence verifies current source values.
  • Flagged for Review: partial coverage, fee scenario, missing fields, unconnected account, no causal news, sampling and persistence limits.

Calculation rules

Prices are fixed-point dollars converted to cents for display. Alert threshold is exactly 10 cents in either direction on the same price basis. Face notional is volume × the API-reported settlement face value; missing inputs are never replaced by $1 or zero. A quoted last price is not an executable ask. A missing market or a passed close time is not a YES/NO result.
