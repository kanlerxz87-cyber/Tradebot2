What this dashboard can and cannot tell you

  • Moves, not causes. The API reports prices and trades, not news. The site never claims a headline caused a move; where a candidate cause is shown it is clearly labelled as a reference and unlinked to the price data.
  • Demo ≠ production. Kalshi states demo prices may not reflect real markets, and the demo carries a subset of markets. Some series are empty here by design.
  • Refresh cadence. Alerts compare consecutive refreshes (default 60 s), not a per-second tape. Intra-minute moves between two refreshes can be missed; the 1-minute candles show them after the fact.
  • Paper trading. Simulated, browser-local, fee-aware. Not a Kalshi demo-account balance — that needs signed API keys.
