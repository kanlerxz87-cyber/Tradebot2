1. Project Mission & Strict 5-Topic Scope

Tradebot2 collects, organizes, and analyzes highest dollar volume prediction contracts on Kalshi across five strictly separated topics only:

  • Gas Prices — U.S. national & state retail regular fuel averages (verified against AAA Fuel Prices ↗).
  • Elections — Major U.S. and international presidential/legislative control races.
  • Culture — Oscars, Grammys, The Game Awards, and major pop-culture milestones.
  • Crypto — High-volume BTC & ETH price threshold contracts.
  • Golf — PGA Tour tournaments & Majors (verified against PGA TOUR ↗).

Events remain organized within their own topic and are tracked from active trading through official YES / NO determination and settlement (Kalshi Settlement Docs ↗).

3. Core Live Trading & Alerting Workflows Implemented

Built for fast, uncluttered trader decision-making across active and resolving markets.

Dual ≥ $0.10 Scrolling Alert Chats
Stream 1 alerts every sudden contract jump or drop ≥ $0.10 across all 5 topics. Stream 2 filters exclusively to opportunities passing our transparent High-ROI scoring model (Score ≥ 68/100, spread ≤ $0.05, depth ≥ 150 contracts).
Interactive Chart + Catalyst Links + Liquidity
Switch X-axis between Seconds, Minutes, and Hours; hover any tick for exact contract pricing ($0.00–$1.00); click verified external catalyst links attached to ≥ $0.10 jumps; inspect available contract counts at every bid/ask price level.
Automated Strategies & Demo Portfolio
Run automated simulated strategies (*Swing Mean-Reversion*, *Momentum Breakout*, *Selective High-ROI*, *Liquidity Imbalance*), categorize trades by topic and setup, resolve open positions YES/NO, and create custom rules based on realized results.
