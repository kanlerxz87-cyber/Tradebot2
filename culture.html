How this topic is selected

The classifier is deliberately narrow. A market that cannot be tied to culture is excluded, not guessed into the topic.

  • Official Kalshi category Entertainment (strongest signal; Culture accepted for legacy compatibility).
  • Keywords: box office, movie, film, album, Grammy, Oscar, Emmy, Tony Award, Billboard, Netflix, TV show, celebrity, Taylor Swift, music, concert, award show, Time Person of the Year.
  • The Entertainment category alone is enough; keywords exist for cross-listed series.

Notes for a trader

  • The collector supplements paginated discovery with targeted series queries (KXOSCARPIC, KXGTAONLINE, KXNETFLIXRANKSHOWGLOBAL, KXTIME) so older open Culture series are included alongside newly listed events.
  • Awards markets can resolve on a ceremony that is streamed or reported by multiple outlets; the named settlement source in the series metadata controls.
  • Officially resolved results for culture events are shown in the log below, with the API record linked.
