Topic analysis — Golf

Golf contracts reprice on per-round outcomes. A 0.10 swing is normal for a field leader stumbling — the swing is real but the market recovers fast. The simulated engine models Masters and US Open leader-style contracts; the live dashboard watches the same market type.

What to watch: Watch the highest-volume golf contracts. The ROI model favors moderately-sized books — thin outlier markets are penalized.
